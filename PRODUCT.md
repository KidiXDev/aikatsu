# Aikatsu

Aikatsu is a cross-platform anime streaming application with Indonesian subtitles and AniList / MAL integration, powered by a shared Rust core backend and native Flutter UI for Android/mobile (and QML for desktop).

## Purpose & Users
- **Primary Audience:** Anime enthusiasts streaming shows with Indonesian subtitles.
- **Key Workflows:** Browsing history / continue watching, multi-source anime searching, episode tracking, video playback with quality switching and Anime4K upscaling, and offline downloading.
- **Positioning:** Fast, ad-free, sleek, native performance powered by Rust with a warm, delightful Japanese anime aesthetic.

## Platform
- `android` (Flutter mobile client)
- Desktop QML client exists in parallel.

## Aesthetic & Visual Direction
- **Theme:** Soft Pastel / Warm Japanese Anime & Sakura Blossom.
- **Palette:** Warm cherry blossom tones (sakura pinks `#ff89b0`, `#ff7aa8`, warm blush `#ffeef4`, deep plum cherry `#3c202a` and soft warm obsidian `#181214`), balanced with cozy lavender accents (`#a39eff`).
- **Surfaces:** Solid, tactile surfaces with warm pill cards, refined rounded geometry, subtle warm borders, and cozy elevation instead of heavy translucent glassmorphism.
- **Motion:** Smooth spring/ease-out micro-animations, bouncy pill navigation tabs, and fluid card feedback.
