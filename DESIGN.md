# Design System: Aikatsu

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Warm Sakura"**

Aikatsu has a warm, cozy Japanese anime look drawn from cherry blossoms (sakura). It builds on Material 3 colour roles, but with its own sakura palette, plum-tinted surfaces instead of neutral greys, and rounded type. Surfaces are **solid, tactile and generously rounded**. Depth comes from stepping through tonal surface layers. There is no translucent glassmorphism.

There are two clients. The Android app (Flutter, `mobile/lib/theme.dart`) and the desktop app (Qt Quick, `desktop/qml/Theme.qml`) share the identity: the sakura world, Google Sans Flex, rounded icon shapes, solid surfaces and soft corners. Each has its **own palette**, generated separately from the same sakura seed, so the hex values differ slightly between them. Token keys are prefixed `android-` or `desktop-`, and neither palette stands in for the other. Desktop motion follows Material 3 Expressive spring curves. Android motion is short `easeOutCubic` ease-outs with a bouncy press on cards.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Warm plum-obsidian dark surfaces and cherry-cream light surfaces, never neutral grey.
- Sakura pink primary, blush secondary and lavender tertiary, in both clients.
- Google Sans Flex everywhere; the desktop also sets the rounded axis (`ROND` 25).
- Solid tonal layering; Android cards add a hairline warm border and a soft diffuse shadow.
- Pill capsules for navigation; 16–20px corners for cards and tiles.

## Colors

The palette is a sakura Material 3 scheme in dark and light forms, generated once per client. The desktop seed is `#ff7aa8` (noted in `Theme.qml`).

### Primary
- **Sakura Glow** (desktop-dark-primary, android-dark-primary): the cherry-petal accent. It marks selection, focus rings, live state, text-button labels and progress. On Android it also fills the selected bottom-nav capsule and acts as `sakuraGlow`.
- **Sakura Rose** (desktop-light-primary, android-light-primary): the same role in light mode, a deeper rose so it holds contrast on cream.
- **Primary Container**: on desktop it is a bright pink (`#ff6fa3` in both modes). On Android it is a deep cherry in dark mode and a pale pink in light mode, and it fills selected chips and segments.

### Secondary
- **Blush Lilac** (secondary): a supporting accent, used for the dialog header icon on desktop.
- **Blush Container** (secondary-container): the desktop **selection wash**. It fills the NavRail pill, the Settings room highlight, the segmented-control highlight, selected mode tiles, Tonal buttons and the state band.

### Tertiary
- **Warm Lavender** (tertiary / tertiary-container): the cool counterweight, used for badges such as the episode-count tag on poster cards and Android's tertiary pills.

### Neutral
- **Warm Obsidian / Cherry Cream** (surface): the app background.
- **Surface ladder** (surface-container-low → highest): tonal layers for depth. On desktop, low is the Settings room column, container is setting groups and menus, high is dialogs, and highest is segmented tracks, keycaps and inactive icon tiles.
- **Soft Cream / Warm Ink** (on-surface) and **Muted Rose** (on-surface-variant): primary and secondary text.
- **Warm Hairline** (outline-variant): dividers and borders. Desktop setting rows use it at 0.6 opacity. Android card borders use it at 0.45 (dark) and 0.6 (light), and dividers at 0.35.
- **Success** exists only on desktop (`success`); Android hardcodes `#7fdc9b` once in search.

### Named Rules
**The Two Palettes Rule.** Take colours from the platform you are building for: `Theme.palette.*` on desktop, `Theme.of(context).colorScheme` on Android. Never copy a hex from one client into the other.

**The Selection Wash Rule.** On desktop, selection is a `secondaryContainer` fill with `onSecondaryContainer` content. Primary is kept for focus rings, live state and the active room's icon squircle.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Google Sans Flex. It is bundled on both clients: on desktop as family `"Google Sans Flex"` from `desktop/assets/fonts`, on Android as family `GoogleSansFlex` declared in `pubspec.yaml`.
**Icons:** on desktop, Material Symbols Rounded, a bundled variable font where `FILL` animates from 0 to 1 on selection and `GRAD` is -25 in dark mode. On Android, Flutter's `Icons.*_rounded` set (the Material Icons rounded style).

**Character:** One rounded, friendly sans for every role. Weight and size carry the hierarchy; there is no second family.

### Hierarchy
On desktop, sizes are Qt point sizes. Every role sets `ROND` 25 and an optical size equal to the point size.
- **Display** (600, 42 / 34pt): the largest hero text.
- **Headline** (600 at 28pt; 550 at 24 / 20pt): page titles. "Settings" uses headline.large and room titles use headline.medium.
- **Title** (500, 17 / 13 / 11pt): group titles, room names, tile names.
- **Body** (400, 12 / 10.5 / 9.5pt): row labels (large), detail text (medium), hint and status lines (small).
- **Label** (500, 10.5 / 9.5 / 8.5pt): buttons, segments, keycaps.

Android does not define a text-theme ramp. It sets the family globally, and fixes sizes only for the app-bar title (20/600), filled buttons (14/600), text buttons (13/600), and chips and segments (13/500).

## Layout

The desktop uses a 4-based spacing scale (4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 28, 48) for both `spacing` and `padding`. `padding` has no 48 step.

**Desktop Settings** is arranged as rooms:
- A 280px room column on `surfaceContainerLow` sits on the left.
- The open room on the right scrolls on its own, with content capped at 760px wide and 28px padding.
- Within a room the order is: header (icon squircle and title), then the state band, then grouped settings, with 28px between blocks.

These measurements belong to the pattern and are not Theme tokens.

Android sets bottom-nav item padding (16px horizontal when selected, 10px otherwise) so that five items fit in 360dp.

## Elevation & Depth

Both clients get depth mainly from tonal surface layering; shadows are a soft supplement.

- **Android:** cards have elevation 0, plus a 1px warm hairline border (`cardBorder`) and a diffuse `cardShadow`. A lighter `softShadow` is available. Dialogs, sheets and snackbars use Material elevation (6, 8, 4).
- **Desktop:** settings groups and bands are flat and borderless; the tonal step is the separation. The `Elevation` component draws a Material-style shadow at dp levels 0/1/3/6/8/12 and is used for poster cards (1, rising to 4 on hover), menus (2), dialogs (3) and a few hero and player surfaces.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Android card shadow:** black at 0.35 (dark) or 0.05 (light), 18px blur, 6px y-offset, -2px spread.
- **Android soft shadow:** black at 0.25 (dark) or 0.03 (light), 10px blur, 3px y-offset.
- **Desktop Elevation:** shadow colour at 0.7 alpha, with blur `(dp*5)^0.7` and y-offset `dp/2`.

### Named Rules
**The Solid Surface Rule.** No frosted glass and no illegible translucent layers over content. Translucent black or white is only for player overlays drawn over video.

## Shapes

The corner language is consistent and continuous: pill capsules for navigation and selection tracks, and 16–20px squircles for cards and tiles.

- **Desktop:** the `Theme.rounding` scale runs 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 28, 32, 48, with `full` (10000) for pills.
  - Settings groups and bands use 20.
  - Tiles, menus and the room highlight use 16.
  - Icon squircles use 12–16.
  - Dialogs use 28.
  - Chips and snackbars use 8.
  - Buttons morph from 16 at rest to 12 when checked and 8 when pressed.
- **Android:**
  - `pillRadius` is 999, used for buttons (stadium), chips, segments, badges and the nav capsule.
  - `cardRadius` is 20, input fields 18, poster and episode tiles 18, BouncyTap's default 16, dialogs 24, bottom sheets 28 at the top, snackbars 16.

Selection shapes differ between the two desktop navigation surfaces, and both are current:
- The NavRail selection is a pill (`rounding.full`, 56×32).
- The Settings room highlight is a 16px squircle (`rounding.large`) the full height of the 60px row.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Desktop** (`TextButton` on `ButtonBase`): three types.
  - **Filled:** primary / onPrimary.
  - **Tonal:** secondaryContainer / onSecondaryContainer.
  - **Text:** transparent with a primary label.
  - Padding is 8×12 and the label is label.large. The corners morph (16, then 12 checked, 8 pressed) over DefaultEffects.
  - Disabled is onSurface at 0.1 fill with 0.38 content.
- **Android:** a stadium shape. Filled buttons are 14/600 with 12×20 padding; text buttons are 13/600 with 8×14 padding.

**The One-Button Rule.** A settings row or band has at most one Tonal button, for its main action. Secondary actions sit beside it as Text buttons. Filled buttons are not used in desktop Settings.

### Chips
- **Desktop:** 8px corners. Unselected chips are transparent with a 1px outlineVariant border. Selected chips are filled with their tint and have no border.
- **Android:** a stadium on surfaceContainer with the card border. Selected chips are primaryContainer, and no checkmark is shown.

### Cards / Containers
- **Android:** surfaceContainer, 20px corners, `cardBorder`, `cardShadow`, elevation 0.
- **Desktop:** settings groups are on surfaceContainer with 20px corners and no border. Their rows are separated by outlineVariant hairlines at 0.6 opacity, inset 20px from each side.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Desktop** (`StyledTextField`): outlined with 8px corners. Focus and error colour the outline primary or error.
- **Android:** filled surfaceContainerHigh with 18px corners and the card border. Focus is a 1.5px primary border.

### Navigation
- **Desktop NavRail:**
  - The selected destination gets a secondaryContainer pill (56×32, full radius) whose width animates, and its icon fills.
  - The state layer is a full-radius onSurface wash.
- **Android bottom nav:** the selected item is a primary-filled capsule (999 radius) that animates padding and colour over 260ms `easeOutCubic` inside a BouncyTap.

### Segmented Control (desktop)
- **Component:** `SegmentedControl` (`desktop/qml/components/SegmentedControl.qml`), plus the fixed-width `Segmented` variant in Settings.
- **Track:** a pill on surfaceContainerHighest, 36px tall with a 4px inset.
- **Segments:** fixed 72px width in Settings (`segWidth: 72`), or auto-sized to content (with optional leading icon and count pill) on browsing and management surfaces.
- **Highlight:** a secondaryContainer pill that slides and resizes to the selected segment over FastSpatial (350ms, expressive spring).
- **Keyboard and accessibility:** Left/Right change the value, focus shows a 2px primary ring, and each segment exposes the RadioButton role.
- **When to use:** in place of chip rows for single-choice settings and view filters (Watch history, Watchlist status, Downloads filter, AnimePage episode filter, DownloadDialog mode/quality).

Android's equivalent is the themed `SegmentedButton`: a stadium on surfaceContainer, with primaryContainer for the selected segment.

### State Band (desktop)
- **Component:** `StateBand` (`desktop/qml/components/StateBand.qml`).
- **Purpose:** the first block in each Settings room and management/collection surface (Library, Downloads, Plugins, Folders). It shows what is true right now (what plays, storage used, sources on, active downloads, collection summary, account, app identity) together with its primary actions.
- **Fill:** `secondaryContainer` washed to 0.4 alpha in dark mode and 0.5 in light mode.
- **Shape:** 20px corners, 20px padding, 16px gaps.
- **Rule:** it must never read as one more settings or list card.

### Room List (desktop Settings)
- **Column:** 280px on surfaceContainerLow, under the headline.large "Settings" title.
- **Rows:** each room is 60px tall with 4px gaps. It shows a 36px icon squircle (12px corners), a title.medium name and a body.small **live status line**. The status line turns `error` coloured on failure.
- **Selected room:**
  - A 16px secondaryContainer highlight slides over FastSpatial.
  - The active room's squircle turns primary with an onPrimary icon, and the icon fill animates over DefaultEffects.
- **Keyboard and accessibility:** Up/Down move between rooms. The highlight shows a 2px primary focus ring. Roles are PageTabList and PageTab.

### Setting Row (desktop)
- **Anatomy:** an optional 16px filled icon, then a label in body.large, an optional hint or value line in body.small onSurfaceVariant, and one trailing control (switch, segmented control, or the one-button pair).
- **Size:** at least 60px tall, with 20px left and 16px right insets and hairline dividers between rows.

### Mode Tile (desktop)
- **Tiles:** described tiles in a grid, 84px tall with 16px corners and title.medium name plus body.small description.
- **States:** a tile is surfaceContainer by default and secondaryContainer when selected, with a filled `check_circle`. Focus shows a 2px primary ring.
- **Sharpening grid:** Off sits full width above the nine modes.

### BouncyTap (Android)
The tactile press used on cards and nav items. The tile scales to 0.965 over 140ms with `easeOutCubic` and springs back on release, with an ink ripple clipped to its radius (16px by default).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** take every colour, radius and spacing value from the platform's theme: `Theme.palette`, `Theme.rounding`, `Theme.spacing` and `Theme.padding` on desktop, and the ColorScheme and `context.aikatsu` on Android.
- **Do** animate desktop motion through `Anim` / `CAnim`: FastSpatial (350ms) for sliding highlights, DefaultEffects (200ms) for icon fill and radius, SlowEffects (300ms) for colour.
- **Do** animate Android motion with short `easeOutCubic` transitions (140–300ms) and BouncyTap on tappable cards.
- **Do** follow the One-Button Rule in desktop Settings: one Tonal main action, Text buttons beside it.
- **Do** lead each desktop Settings room with a state band, then lay settings out as grouped setting rows with one trailing control each.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use heavy frosted glass or translucent layers over content.
- **Don't** copy hex values between the Android and desktop palettes.
- **Don't** use Filled buttons in desktop Settings.
- **Don't** fill selected rows, segments or tiles with primary on desktop; selection is the secondaryContainer wash (primary marks focus, live state and the active room's icon squircle).
- **Don't** use chip rows for single-choice settings on desktop; use the segmented control.
