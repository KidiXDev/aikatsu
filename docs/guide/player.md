# Settle in and press play

The player remembers your place, supports quality changes, and can move on to the next episode for you. Some controls depend on what the video actually contains.

## Quality and playback

Set a preferred quality in Settings, or choose a different one from the player. Aikatsu remembers your quality choice. If the exact resolution isn't available, the source can fall back to another one.

The player also has playback speed, volume, and audio and subtitle track controls. Track selection only helps when the stream exposes multiple tracks. Indonesian subtitles are often part of the video itself, so there may be no separate subtitle track to switch off.

If playback fails before it starts, the player can try other available qualities. A broken source or expired stream can still need a retry or another source.

## Pick up where you left off

Aikatsu saves watch progress locally. Reopen a show from Home to continue, or choose an episode from its details page. When a resume choice appears, you can carry on from your saved position or start again.

Progress stays on the device where you watched it. Aikatsu doesn't sync your history to an AniList or MyAnimeList account.

## Skip openings and endings

Use **Skip intro & outro** in Settings for automatic skips, or use the player's skip control when a segment is available. Desktop also has the **S** shortcut.

Skip times come from AniSkip and aren't available for every anime or episode. An ending skip also depends on where you are in the episode and whether there's a next episode.

## Keep watching

With **Autoplay next episode** enabled, the player shows a short countdown at the end of an episode. You can cancel it if you're done for the night. Previous and next episode controls are also available when another episode exists.

## Desktop shortcuts

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Space or K | Play or pause |
| Left / Right, or J / L | Jump back or forward 5 seconds |
| Shift + Left / Right, or Shift + J / L | Jump back or forward 85 seconds |
| Up / Down | Change volume |
| M | Mute or unmute |
| F, or double-click the video | Toggle fullscreen |
| N / P | Next or previous episode |
| S | Skip the current opening or ending segment |
| Esc | Leave fullscreen first, then close the player |
| Ctrl+L | Open the app log |

## Android controls

Tap the video to show or hide controls. Double-tap the left or right side to jump back or forward 10 seconds. Use the seek bar to move further through the episode.

Double-tapping the middle toggles play and pause.

Long-press an episode or anime card outside the player for extra actions where available.

## Want sharper video?

Open the Anime4K controls in the player or Settings. Start with Standard or Light, then see whether your device has room for a stronger preset. The [Anime4K guide](./anime4k.md) explains the choices.
