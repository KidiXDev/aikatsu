# Find something to watch

If you already know the title, head to Search. If you don't, Home gives you a few places to start.

## Browse Home

Home combines your watch history with discovery shelves from AniList:

- **Continue** shows unfinished shows and where you left off.
- **Recent** lets you revisit what you've watched, including finished shows.
- **Out today** shows recently aired episodes.
- **Up next** shows upcoming airings.
- **Trending now** and the upcoming-anime shelf help you find something new.

Discovery follows AniList's schedule. An episode appearing there doesn't mean your selected source has uploaded it yet.

Open a discovery card for its details, then search for the show in your installed sources. Discovery itself doesn't provide a video stream.

## Search several sources at once

Enter at least two characters and submit. Aikatsu searches enabled plugins in parallel and adds results as they arrive.

The source filters show which sites are still loading, how many results they returned, and whether something failed. Use a filter to focus on one source. On mobile, tapping a failed source can show its error.

Try another spelling, a shorter title, or the Japanese title if a search comes back empty. The app turns hyphens and underscores into spaces before sending a query to a source.

A site that repeatedly fails may be skipped for a short cooldown. That keeps one broken source from slowing down every search.

## Read the anime details

The details page brings together the source's episode list and metadata such as:

- Cover art, synopsis, score, and genres.
- Episode titles and available episode information.
- Filler and recap markers when that data exists.
- Your saved episode progress.

Metadata comes from AniList, with Jikan used as a fallback and for episode information. Source pages and metadata services don't always agree about a title or episode count. Missing metadata doesn't automatically mean the video source is broken.

## Find an episode and track what you've watched

Use **Jump to…** to narrow a long episode list by its starting digits. For example, entering `12` can show episodes 12 and 120 through 129. Clear the field to see everything again. The sort control switches between oldest-first and newest-first order.

An episode's context menu lets you mark it as watched or unwatched without playing it. On Android, long-press the episode; on desktop, open its context menu. Those marks update your local progress and help Home choose what to continue.

The details page also has refresh and download actions. Refresh fetches a fresh episode list and metadata instead of relying on their normal cached responses.

## Choose which sites Search uses

Open **Settings > Plugins** and switch individual sources on or off. Turning one off keeps its repo installed, but leaves it out of searches.

If a source goes missing after a repo was removed, add that repo again. Existing history can still refer to the old source, but online playback needs the plugin to be installed.
